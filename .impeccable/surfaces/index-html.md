Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: people who generate many AI images across ChatGPT, Gemini, Flow, Google AI Studio, Codex, Grok, Jimeng, in Chinese or English, deciding whether to install a free local library.
Action: download the macOS app (Windows preview and the Chrome extension secondary), straight from the first viewport, with version and size from the release feed.
Proof and content: the real interface and the user's own approved work; honest provenance labels; no testimonials, counts or benchmarks exist and none may be invented. Demo data is labelled as demo.

Memorable moment: the hero prints itself — the poster images arrive as cyan, magenta and yellow plates that slide into register, the black interface lands, then the fluorescent spot unit prints the selection ring, the proof note and the download block.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a press run of GravityPort's own job: every image is printed with its slug — Prompt, source, round — in the margin, so the product's mechanism (context travels with the image) is how the page is built. It refuses the dark SaaS hero with a glowing tilted screenshot over a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool uncoated stock #F2F3F0 as ground; key-black floods #121212 as live areas that carry the dark product; one fluorescent spot ink #39FF14 for the primary action, proof marks and selection; CMYK only in colour bars and the plate reveal. Hairline crop marks, registration targets, colour-bar strips, slug lines in Fragment Mono for data only; display in Archivo condensed black and Noto Sans SC Black; proof notes in a Long Cang hand. Provenance drawn by rule type: solid recorded, dashed observed/unverified, gap unavailable.

STORY: The visitor learns GravityPort pulls their AI images from the tools they already use into one library on their own computer, keeps each image's Prompt, source and rounds, organizes by dragging and by automatic grouping and stacking, finds every version again, stays private and free; believes it by watching it happen on real work with honest labels; downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed press sheet. Stock margin carries the top slug (mark, wordmark, nav, 中/EN, compact download) and bottom slug; trim marks at four corners; colour bar down the left edge doubles as source legend. Inside trim, a black live area: left ~40% the headline at display scale, one-sentence subhead, the fluorescent download block with platform, version and size beneath it, and a secondary link to the demos; right ~60% the GravityPort window at near life size bleeding off the right trim, one poster circled in fluorescent marker with that image's real round instruction handwritten beside it; under the live area the circled image's slug prints its provenance.

FORM: Press sheet / press proof — position 4 of my ordered list (1 contact sheet, 2 observatory plate archive, 3 studio pin wall, 4 press proof, 5 darkroom safelight, 6 specimen drawer, 7 container port).
